# Dashboard Design: Invoices (Money Coming In)

## 🎯 KPIs (Quick Numbers)
* **Total Unpaid Money:** Total amount customers currently owe.
* **Overdue Money:** Amount past the due date. Needs immediate action.
* **Money Collected This Month:** Total cash received in current month.
* **Average Days to Pay:** How fast customers usually pay after receiving invoice.

## 📊 Charts / Graphs (Trends)
* **Money Expected vs. Money Overdue (Bar Chart):** Compare what is owed now vs what is late, broken down by month.
* **Top 5 Customers Who Owe Money (Donut Chart):** Visual breakdown of biggest unpaid amounts.
* **Collection Trend (Line Chart):** Show if customers are paying faster or slower over last 6 months.
