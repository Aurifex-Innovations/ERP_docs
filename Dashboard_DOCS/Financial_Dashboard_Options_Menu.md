# Financial Dashboard Options Menu (For Final Selection)

*Review these options. Pick the KPIs, Charts, Tables, and Alerts you want for each module. The "Purpose" for each option has been expanded to explain exact business value.*

---

## Module 1: Invoices (Money Coming In)

### KPI Options (Choose 4-6)
1. **Total Unpaid Money:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Gives an immediate snapshot of all capital currently tied up in pending invoices. Helps the business owner understand exactly how much cash is floating outside the bank account.
2. **Total Overdue Money:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Highlights the exact dollar amount that is past due. This is critical for identifying cash flow bottlenecks and triggering immediate collection efforts.
3. **Average Days to Pay (DSO):** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Tracks the average number of days it takes for customers to pay after receiving an invoice. A rising number indicates worsening collection efficiency, which can threaten payroll or operational funding.
4. **Collection Effectiveness Index:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Measures the percentage of money collected in a given time period against the total money available for collection. A clear indicator of the collection team's performance.
5. **Money Collected This Month:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Shows total cash actually realized this month. Crucial for comparing against monthly expense budgets to ensure the business remains solvent.
6. **Bad Debt Written Off:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Tracks money the business has given up on collecting. Essential for understanding financial losses due to poor customer credit.

### Chart Options (Choose 3-4)
1. **Aging Buckets (Column Chart):** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Groups overdue money into time buckets (e.g., 1-30 days, 31-60 days, 90+ days late). This visualization instantly shows how much debt is aging into high-risk territory where it might never be paid.
2. **Money Expected vs. Money Overdue (Stacked Bar Chart):** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Compares the amount of money due in the future against money already late. Helps owners forecast near-term cash shortages if the overdue portion is too large.
3. **Top 5 Customers Owe Most (Donut Chart):** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Identifies revenue concentration risk. If one customer holds 50% of the unpaid money, the business is highly vulnerable if that customer defaults.
4. **Invoices Issued vs. Paid (Line Chart):** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Tracks the historical trend of billing volume versus collection volume. Shows if the business is generating paper revenue but failing to convert it to actual cash.

### Table Options (Choose 2-3)
1. **Urgent Action: Severely Overdue Customers:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Provides a targeted hit-list for the collections team. Focuses only on invoices past a certain threshold (e.g., 45 days) so staff know exactly who to call today.
2. **Coming Due This Week:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Lists customers whose payments are expected in the next 7 days. Useful for soft-reminder emails and near-term cash flow planning.
3. **High-Risk Customers:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Displays customers who frequently pay late or have bounced checks in the past. Alerts staff to demand upfront payments for future work.

### Alert Options (Choose 2-3)
1. **Invoice 60+ Days Late:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Triggers a high-priority warning to the business owner when a significant payment reaches critical delinquency, prompting legal or collection agency escalation.
2. **Top Customer Stopped Paying:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Notifies management if a traditionally reliable, high-volume customer misses a payment, often signaling a lost account or bankruptcy risk.

## Module 2: Bills (Money Going Out)

### KPI Options (Choose 4-6)
1. **Total Unpaid Bills:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Summarizes all current financial obligations to vendors. Critical for understanding the total liability the business must cover in the near future.
2. **Overdue Bills:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Highlights vendor invoices that missed their payment deadline. Vital for preventing late fees, interest charges, or supply chain cutoffs.
3. **Upcoming Bills (Next 7 Days):** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Projects exactly how much cash must leave the bank account this week. Allows the owner to verify if current cash balances can cover immediate needs.
4. **Total Expenses This Month:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Tracks the total volume of money spent in the current month. Used to monitor burn rate and ensure spending stays within budget.
5. **Early Payment Discounts Available:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Calculates the total money the business could save if they pay certain bills early (e.g., 2% net 10 terms). Directly impacts profit margins.

### Chart Options (Choose 3-4)
1. **Spending by Category (Donut Chart):** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Visually breaks down where money is going (e.g., Software, Rent, Inventory, Marketing). Quickly identifies bloated expense categories that need budget cuts.
2. **Cash Need Forecast (Bar Chart):** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Maps out required payments week-by-week for the next month. Helps owners time their own collections to match the dates when major bills are due.
3. **Top 5 Most Expensive Vendors (Column Chart):** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Highlights the suppliers taking the largest chunk of the budget. Useful for identifying where to negotiate better rates or volume discounts.
4. **Historical Spend Trend (Line Chart):** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Shows if the business is gradually spending more money over time. Essential for catching lifestyle inflation or hidden recurring costs.

### Table Options (Choose 2-3)
1. **Urgent Bills to Pay Today:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* An actionable list of bills that will incur penalties or cause service interruptions if not paid by 5:00 PM today.
2. **Bills Eligible for Early Discount:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* A money-saving list. Shows bills that offer a discount if paid before a specific date, allowing the accounts payable team to optimize cash utilization.
3. **Recurring Subscriptions:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Lists all automated monthly expenses. Helps owners easily spot and cancel unused software or services to trim the fat.

### Alert Options (Choose 2-3)
1. **Critical Service Bill Overdue:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Sends an immediate alert if a bill for essential services (like AWS servers or electricity) is overdue, preventing catastrophic business shutdowns.
2. **Duplicate Bill Detected:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Warns the user if a vendor submits two identical invoices, preventing the business from accidentally paying the same bill twice.
3. **Unusual Expense Spike:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Alerts management if a normally cheap category suddenly sees a massive expense, potentially catching fraud or unauthorized employee spending.

## Module 3: Payments (Cash Movement)

### KPI Options (Choose 4-6)
1. **Total Cash In (This Month):** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Shows the exact amount of liquid cash that has hit the bank account. This is the ultimate truth of business revenue, stripping away unpaid paper invoices.
2. **Total Cash Out (This Month):** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Shows the exact amount of cash that has left the bank account. Represents the true operational burn rate of the business.
3. **Net Cash Flow:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Cash In minus Cash Out. This single number tells the owner if the bank account is growing (positive) or shrinking (negative) this month.
4. **Failed / Bounced Payments:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Counts transactions that were rejected by banks or credit cards. Critical for identifying fraud, expired cards, or bad checks early.
5. **Processing Fees Paid:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Totals the money lost to payment gateways (like Stripe or PayPal). Helps owners realize if they need to negotiate better transaction rates.

### Chart Options (Choose 3-4)
1. **Cash Flow Trajectory (Waterfall Chart):** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Visually demonstrates how the starting bank balance was impacted by various inflows and outflows to reach the ending balance. Makes complex cash movements easy to understand.
2. **Cash In vs Cash Out Trend (Double Line Chart):** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Tracks whether the business consistently brings in more than it spends over time. Crossing lines (Out > In) visually warn of impending cash crunches.
3. **Payment Method Breakdown (Donut Chart):** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Shows how customers prefer to pay (ACH, Credit Card, Wire). Helps the business optimize payment systems and reduce high processing fees.

### Table Options (Choose 2-3)
1. **Recent Failed Transactions:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Provides an actionable list of payments that bounced, complete with customer contact info and failure reasons, so staff can immediately request new payment methods.
2. **Unreconciled Bank Deposits:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Lists lump sums deposited in the bank that haven't been matched to specific invoices yet. Ensures the accounting records perfectly match reality.
3. **Large Cash Outflows:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Acts as an audit log for major spending. Lists any single transaction over a certain dollar amount for owner review and approval verification.

### Alert Options (Choose 2-3)
1. **Low Bank Balance Warning:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Triggers when projected cash drops below a safety threshold (e.g., $10,000), giving the owner time to secure a loan or push collections before bouncing checks.
2. **High Volume of Failed Payments:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Warns if the payment gateway is rejecting an unusual number of transactions, which could indicate a technical outage or a coordinated fraud attack.
3. **Unexpected Large Withdrawal:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Sends an immediate security alert to the owner's phone if a massive sum is withdrawn, protecting against embezzlement or bank hacking.

## Module 4: Financial Health (Overall Ledger)

### KPI Options (Choose 4-6)
1. **Net Profit:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* The ultimate bottom line. Shows exactly how much money the business actually made after every single expense is deducted from revenue.
2. **Gross Profit Margin:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Shows the percentage of revenue left after paying for the direct costs of delivering a product/service. Essential for evaluating if the core business model is viable.
3. **Total Assets (What We Own):** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Calculates the total value of everything the business owns (cash, equipment, inventory). Used by banks to determine the company's worth and borrowing power.
4. **Total Liabilities (What We Owe):** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Calculates the total debt the business holds (loans, unpaid taxes, vendor debt). Critical for monitoring long-term solvency risk.
5. **Debt-to-Equity Ratio:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Compares how much of the business is funded by debt vs owner investment. High numbers warn that the business is dangerously over-leveraged.

### Chart Options (Choose 3-4)
1. **Profit vs Revenue Trend (Line Chart):** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Shows if rising sales are actually resulting in rising profits. Prevents the common trap where a business grows revenue but operational costs eat all the gains.
2. **Asset vs Liability Balance (Stacked Column Chart):** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* A visual representation of the balance sheet. Instantly shows if the business owns significantly more than it owes, indicating strong financial health.
3. **Top Expenses vs Top Income Sources (Tornado Chart):** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Compares the biggest revenue drivers directly against the biggest cost centers. Helps owners see which products fund the business and which departments drain it.

### Table Options (Choose 2-3)
1. **Most Profitable Segments:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Ranks products, services, or locations by their net profit contribution. Guides the owner on where to focus marketing and expansion efforts.
2. **Highest Cost Centers:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Lists the departments or categories burning the most cash. Focuses cost-cutting measures on the areas where they will have the most impact.
3. **Monthly Income Statement Summary:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* A simplified P&L table showing Top Line Revenue, Cost of Goods, Operating Expenses, and Bottom Line Profit. Provides a quick financial checkup without deep accounting reports.

### Alert Options (Choose 2-3)
1. **Profit Margin Dropped Below Target:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Warns the owner if the cost of doing business is creeping up faster than prices, signaling that a price increase or cost-cut is urgently needed.
2. **Liability Exceeds Asset Warning:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* A critical red-flag alert indicating the business is technically insolvent (it owes more than it is worth), requiring immediate restructuring.
3. **Tax Reserve Too Low:** 
   - *Detailed Purpose:* Warns if the cash set aside in the bank is less than the estimated quarterly tax bill, preventing devastating surprises at tax time.
